Before going on the journey, you have to gather your team... 🧙 
Below you will find a couple of tools that will help you create your spells in the world of Dataverse. 

**Visual Studio Code** 

This IDE is free and full of possibilities. It will be our main workspace in the future: https://code.visualstudio.com/download 

**PAC CLI** 

Scripts that will help you create plugins, PCFs, and many other things: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/developer/cli/introduction?tabs=windows 

**XRM Toolbox** 

A tool created by the community with lots of helpful utilities: https://www.xrmtoolbox.com/ 
After downloading all of the above, you are ready to start your journey!
